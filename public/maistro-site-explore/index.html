€3,450Actual food usage

The €450 difference is not automatically recoverable profit. Check counts, recipes, waste, deliveries and timing before drawing a conclusion.

Forecast sales and stock needs+

Maistro AI considers weather, events, holidays, sales history and other conditions to forecast demand and stock requirements. The result gives purchasing a forward view, rather than relying on last week’s order.

Central ordering+

Manage stock purchasing centrally, with supplier orders and internal venue orders in the stock workflow. Use forecast demand and current stock to plan requirements, then track orders through booking and receipt. Internal ordering connects a requesting venue with a supplying venue.

Recipes, sizes and portions+

Connect products and modifiers to the ingredients and quantities they use. A small pizza, a large pizza and an extra topping can consume different amounts of the same ingredient. Those quantities connect sales to expected stock usage.

Theoretical versus actual usage+

Theoretical usage is what recorded sales should have consumed according to recipes. Actual usage comes from stock movement and counts. For a simple period without transfers or other adjustments, it is opening stock plus purchases minus closing stock. Compare the same period and units to find the gap.

Counts, deliveries and waste+

Keep receipts, counts, waste and transfers up to date so the stock picture is explainable. Supplier lead times matter when deciding when to order: the weekly example above shows how a forecast shortage can be spotted before service.

WHY IT MATTERS

Plan stock around expected demand and get a clearer view of where food margin goes.

Central ordering here means stock purchasing and internal venue orders. The forecast supports the plan; counts and stock movements explain the result.

Kitchen coordination
